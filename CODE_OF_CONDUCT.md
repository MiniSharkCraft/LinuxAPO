# Community Code of Conduct

LinuxAPO welcomes people who want to learn, report bugs, improve documentation, or contribute code.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Focus feedback on ideas, evidence, and code rather than personal attacks.
- Be patient with newcomers and people with different experience levels.
- Respect privacy and do not publish someone else's personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and avoid spam or harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, unwanted personal attention, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not welcome.

## Reporting and moderation

If a discussion or comment violates these expectations, use GitHub's reporting features or contact the repository maintainers through an appropriate private channel. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting exploit details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed. Reports should be handled with care and without unnecessary disclosure of private information.

This policy applies to LinuxAPO's project-managed spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.
