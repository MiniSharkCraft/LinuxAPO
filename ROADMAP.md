# SkyAPO roadmap

Release planning is summarized in [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md); this root file is the detailed historical acceptance/evidence tracker toward 1.0.0. LinuxAPO 0.9.0 remains the published daily-use preview. The port and its pinned official Equalizer APO submodule are tracked separately, and realtime proof in `docs/REALTIME.md` remains a regression gate. Roadmap versions are targets, not claims; only tested acceptance criteria advance a milestone. Review updated 2026-10-14. The probe measures only synthetic producer-to-consumer callback delay, not physical ADC or total application end-to-end latency.

- Physical microphone unplug/replug and verification that desktop clients automatically follow source changes; synthetic PipeWire removal/reappearance passes, but hardware manipulation/client behavior is not proven here.
- Dependency-populated isolated Debian/RPM install/uninstall transactions and Arch ALPM hook execution. None of this verifies Debian/RPM dependency resolution or booted-root Arch ALPM hook behavior.
- Hosted clean-root/reboot lifecycle validation beyond the verified Arch package transaction; physical reboot and systemd-booted install/remove behavior remain unproven.
- Legal/trademark and distribution clearance, especially VST2/FST and Qt/plugin bundle notices; keep experimental VST2 disabled in default/package builds.
- Broad third-party plugin/yabridge compatibility, realtime guarantees for arbitrary plugins, process isolation/crash containment, auxiliary buses and plugin-native automation/state parity.

These deferred gates remain in the 1.0.0 backlog. Do not mark the historical unchecked hardware, clean-install, legal, or broad compatibility criteria complete merely to ship 0.9.0.
